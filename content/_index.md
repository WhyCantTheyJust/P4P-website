At Policy 4 People, we think government has a role in improving people’s lives, and a responsibility to do so in a way that is both effective and fair. 
We also believe that the strength of societies lies in their inclusiveness and the participation of their people in civic life. We believe in empowering people to engage, through democratic processes, in issues of importance to them.

We are dismayed by the lack of depth in policy slogans and political commentary, and so we look at important policies and unpack what they really mean, and what their consequences are for real people. Our podcast [Why Can't They Just?](https://whycanttheyjust.com.au/) analyses policy, not for its impact on politicians and their careers, but for its impact on real people and their lives and livelihoods.
