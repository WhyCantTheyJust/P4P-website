Policy 4 People promotes democracy and civic engagement. This includes, but is not limited to:

Policy translation and dialogue
: We facilitate a two-way flow of understanding between government and the public. We translate policy and its impacts for citizens, and we channel public and stakeholder perspectives back into policy processes.

Community capability building
: We build civic skills, awareness, and confidence across communities, using diverse approaches. We tailor our approaches to suit communities of varying demographics, backgrounds, geographies, cultures, and socioeconomic circumstances, in order to empower their participation in democratic life.

Grassroots organising
: We organise and support grassroots community participation in civic and policy issues, as a foundation of a healthy and functioning democracy.
